### Speaker
Tobias (Tobi) Löw is a postdoctoral scholar advised by Prof. Siddhartha Srinivasa in the Personal Robotics Lab at the University of Washington. He obtained his Ph.D. from EPFL in 2025 and his Bachelor's and Master's degrees from ETH Zürich. His research focuses on geometrically coherent representations for robotics—in particular geometric algebra for robot manipulation, control, and optimization—to support adaptability and robustness in unstructured, human-centric environments.
