abstract: >
    Soft robotics offers new opportunities for designing robotic systems that can interact safely and effectively with people while adapting to complex and unstructured environments. In this talk, I will discuss my research at the intersection of soft robotics, haptics, human–robot interaction, and field robotics, with an emphasis on how compliance, novel sensing and actuation, and reconfigurable designs can enable new forms of physical interaction and adaptation. I will highlight lessons from developing robotic systems across a range of applications, from communicating information through touch to operating in challenging real-world environments. More broadly, the talk will explore how the design of a robot’s body, sensing, and interaction capabilities can be considered together to create systems that are better suited to the people, tasks, and environments in which they operate.
